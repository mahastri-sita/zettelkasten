# Community Labels

Status: provisional navigation labels, not a substantive synthesis or user-approved taxonomy.

Coverage: `200/200` communities.
Confidence distribution: `63` high, `21` medium, `116` low.

The complete mapping and evidence basis are in `community-label-proposals.json`; `.graphify_labels.json` is the mapping applied to both graph exports.

Low-confidence labels are intentionally phrased as thin navigation descriptors and should not be treated as claims about the literature.

# AGENTS.md

## Scope

These rules apply to `topic-notes/level-2/`. Inherit `topic-notes/AGENTS.md` and the root `AGENTS.md`.

Level-2 notes represent narrower topics, concepts, mechanisms, debates, or research-defined constructs, such as `Urban Cannibalism`, `Accessibility Capitalization`, or `Spatial Mismatch`.

## Creation and Content

- Create a Level-2 note when the concept functions as a real router under the parent creation threshold.
- Use the canonical concept or construct name as the filename.
- Link all relevant Level-1 parents; multiple parents are allowed and often useful.
- Connect Arguments, Synthesis Products, Sources, Calculations, Outputs, and related Level-2 concepts with annotated links.
- State scope only far enough to distinguish the topic from adjacent concepts.
- Do not promote an original concept into a Synthesis Product merely because it has a Topic Note.
- Do not reproduce the reasoning or evidence contained in the linked objects.

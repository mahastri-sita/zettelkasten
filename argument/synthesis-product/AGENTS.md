# AGENTS.md

## Scope

These rules apply to `argument/synthesis-product/`. Inherit `argument/AGENTS.md` and the root `AGENTS.md`.

A Synthesis Product is mature personal synthesis worth preserving and reusing. It is not a literature summary and cannot be generated directly from Source.

## Promotion Gate

AI may nominate a candidate Synthesis Product, but the user decides whether it is promoted. Do not create the file before explicit approval.

A nomination should explain whether the candidate:

- has passed through substantive Argument or an Output grounded in Argument;
- is sufficiently mature and defensible;
- has been tested against material counterevidence;
- has independent reuse value across projects or contexts;
- remains traceable to relevant Source and Calculation objects; and
- states important scope conditions and residual uncertainty.

Do not use polished wording as evidence of maturity.

## Editing

- Edit an existing Synthesis Product only with explicit editing intent.
- Preserve the confirmed synthesis unless the user approves a substantive change.
- If later evidence weakens the synthesis, report the conflict and propose reconsideration. Do not silently downgrade, strengthen, or replace it.
- A confirmed change in intellectual position may also warrant a proposed Contradiction Journey entry.

## Naming

Use a concise, stable name that expresses the reusable synthesis or proposition. Do not use IDs, timestamps, or project-specific status labels as the primary name.

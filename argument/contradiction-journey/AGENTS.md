# AGENTS.md

## Scope

These rules apply to `argument/contradiction-journey/`. Inherit `argument/AGENTS.md` and the root `AGENTS.md`.

This folder stores compressed intellectual trajectory to prevent regression. Its canonical substantive file is `contradiction-journey.md`.

## Authority

- Read relevant portions for substantive research and thinking tasks.
- Do not require it for small technical or administrative tasks.
- AI may propose an entry but must obtain explicit approval before creating or editing the canonical file.
- Never infer that the user's position changed merely because counterevidence was found.

## Inclusion Threshold

Record only a user-confirmed change in interpretation, assumption, framework, research question, or intellectual position. Ordinary counterevidence remains in the relevant Argument until it produces a confirmed change.

An entry should preserve, as available:

- the previous position;
- the confirmed revised position;
- the evidence, contradiction, or reasoning that triggered the change;
- why the previous position was abandoned or bounded;
- remaining uncertainty or conditions; and
- the date of confirmation.

This is not a diary, activity log, changelog, or dump of every disagreement. Keep entries compressed but sufficient to prevent an agent from reviving an abandoned position without understanding why.

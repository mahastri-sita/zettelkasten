# Portal Data APBD dan Transfer ke Daerah

## Dataset Description

The DJPK portal provides filters for APBD period, year, province, and local government, together with displayed budget and realization categories. The accessed page presented national aggregates and reported 546 local governments with APBD data received by SIKD for the displayed 2026 selection.

## Variables or Schema

The portal displays categories including regional revenue, locally generated revenue (PAD), transfers, regional expenditure, personnel expenditure, goods and services, capital expenditure, and regional financing. No raw export was downloaded for this record.

## Coverage and Unit of Observation

- Spatial coverage: national portal with provincial and local-government filters.
- Temporal coverage: selectable annual periods; the displayed period changes with portal state.
- Unit of observation: not fixed here because the portal combines government, account, budget, and realization dimensions.

## Collection or Production Method

The portal page identifies the data as received by SIKD and exposes budget/realization summaries. The underlying collection and validation procedures were not documented in the accessed page.

## Known Limitations

- This is a metadata-only record; no APBD values were downloaded or independently recalculated.
- Portal values and the selected period are dynamic.
- The existing BibTeX note says "Data APBD Desember 2025; diakses April 2026," but the supplied URL currently displays a 2026 selection with realization through August 2026. This record does not silently reconcile those two snapshots.

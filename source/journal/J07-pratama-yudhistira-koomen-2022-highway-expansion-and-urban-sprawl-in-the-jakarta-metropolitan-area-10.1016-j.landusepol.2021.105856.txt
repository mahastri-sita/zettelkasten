the Jakarta Metropolitan Area                             variables. Our results show that improvement in highway access increases urban sprawl within the JMA,
                                                          particularly within 40–50 km radius from the city centre. The results also confirm the existence of highway-led

                                                          enticing evidence that highway expansion leads to a sprawling development of urban areas even within a large
                                                          metropolitan area.

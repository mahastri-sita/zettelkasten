# Spatial Structure and Productivity in US Metropolitan Areas

## AI-Processed Digest

### Core Claims or Contents

The abstract examines whether the monocentricity-polycentricity dimension affects the economic performance of US metropolitan areas. It reports that polycentricity is associated with higher labor productivity in the authors' ordinary least squares and two-stage least-squares models. The abstract also states that a network of nearby smaller cities cannot substitute for the urbanization externalities of one large city (abstract).
